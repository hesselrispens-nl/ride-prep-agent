🛞 Team tyre pressures

How this is calculated

Inputs mirror the SILCA Pro calculator (system weight, surface, measured width, wheel size, casing, speed, weight distribution). Base pressure uses the published 15 % tyre-drop regression (Berto), then surface, casing, speed and wheel-size factors calibrated to SILCA-style outputs. Beach surfaces are not in SILCA — those values are a low-pressure starting point; test on the sand. Always respect rim/tyre maximums; hookless rims are capped at 72 psi (5.0 bar).
